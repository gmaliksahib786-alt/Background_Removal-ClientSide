Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device. This is completely private — no server logs, no data collection.

Why is it slow on first use?

The AI model (~30–40MB) downloads from a CDN and gets cached in your browser. After the first use, it loads instantly from cache — no download needed again.

Which model should I use?

"Best Quality" (isnet_fp16) for portraits, hair detail, and complex edges. "Balanced" for everyday product images. "Fast" or "Mobile" for batch processing or slower devices.

What file format is the output?

Transparent background downloads as PNG (alpha channel). If you choose a background color, it exports as PNG with that solid fill. JPEG output is not supported since JPEG doesn't support transparency.

Is there a file size or image count limit?

No hard limits. Processing is limited only by your device's RAM. Very large images (20MP+) may be slower — the tool auto-caps preview at 1080px for speed while keeping the output at original resolution.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, but use the "Mobile" or "Fast" model on phones for best performance. The isnet models are optimised for desktop CPUs and may take 15–30s on older mobile devices.
